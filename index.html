Questions clubs ask first.

What does it cost?

Pricing depends on numbers, how often you order and which menu you choose. Send us the details and we'll put a quote together.

Can you cover different dietary needs in one order?

Yes. Our range includes halal, vegan, keto and clean options, so a mixed squad can be catered for together. Tell us about any allergies when you enquire.

Where do you deliver?

We prep in Irlam, Greater Manchester. Give us your postcode when you enquire and we'll confirm delivery.

Can we order for a one-off event?

Yes. Tournaments, camps and events are welcome. Tell us the dates and numbers and we'll come back with options.

Is this different from your personal meal prep plans?

Yes. This page is for clubs, academies and facilities ordering for a group. If you want meals for yourself, take a look at our personal meal prep plans.

Questions partners ask first.

What does it cost?

Pricing depends on numbers, how often you order and which menu you choose. Send us the details and we'll put a quote together.

Can you cover different dietary needs in one order?

Yes. Our range includes halal, vegan, keto and clean options, so a mixed group can be catered for together. Tell us about any allergies when you enquire.

Where do you deliver?

We prep at our Irlam HQ in Greater Manchester and deliver on a tracked service, or you can collect. Give us your postcode when you enquire and we'll confirm delivery.

How fresh are the meals?

Meals are prepared fresh every week and arrive in an insulated box that keeps them fresh for up to 48 hours. Sealed, biodegradable containers then keep them fresh for up to a week.

Can we order for a one-off event?

Yes. Tournaments, camps and events are welcome. Tell us the dates and numbers and we'll come back with options.

Is this different from your personal meal plans?

Yes. This page is for clubs, gyms and commercial units ordering for a group. If you want meals for yourself, take a look at our personal meal plans.
